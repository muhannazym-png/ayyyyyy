✧ OUR LITTLE TIMELINE ✧

It started with a message.
Look where we are now.

I remember so many little things. Here are some of the moments that still feel warm when I think about them.

01
11 JULY 2025✦

The start of a little something

We met on a summer day, without knowing how much space we would eventually take up in each other's lives. At first, it was just a conversation. Then I started looking forward to the next one.

It's strange and lovely to think that something so important to me began so simply. If I could visit that day, I would tell that version of me to pay attention — she was meeting someone who would become one of her favourite people.

“I had no idea this would become our story.”
02
10 OCTOBER 2025☾

The call I was so nervous about

We decided to chat for three days before the call. I was so nervous. When October 10th finally came, I was at school and immediately asked to leave my classes just so I could talk to you.

You started so confidently, with so much energy. I was shy and had no idea how I was going to speak. Even when I didn't understand every sentence, even when I couldn't make my own, I was so happy to be on a call with you.

I even loved our silence. Sometimes I didn't know what you had said, and all I could do was go “bebehe” — ahahahaha. When you started repeating after me, I felt this little connection. I loved your voice so much. I didn't understand everything, but I loved listening to you talk.

We talked for so long. I will never forget the word “yacht” that I couldn't understand, or the loud, funny typing sound while I was using the translator. 😅 That day, I also got your WhatsApp number and learned your surname.

That was also the day my mother found out I was talking to you and told me not to fall in love with you. I remember thinking that it was probably already too late.

♫Your voice, my nervous “bebehe,” and a very loud translator.
03
THE LITTLE THINGS ERA✧

We kept finding new things to share

You suggested we try making the humidity-sensor project again. We made a Spotify Blend. You made a scrolling text with my name on the display, and I kept thinking about it because it made me so happy. Then you made a smiley face with “hi” on the display. Such a tiny thing, but I loved it.

We played chess, taught each other words in our languages, and told each other things we hadn't told everyone else — like how you left for another city without telling your parents, and how you took apart your mum's bicycle. We talked about Suits and tried to watch it together on Rave.

Then I finally saw what you looked like. I could only see part of your face in the profile picture, but I was genuinely shocked by how handsome you were. I kept looking at your photo; it filled me with this strange, lovely feeling of delight and affection.

I discovered your photography and admired your pictures so much. We talked about trains, and I loved your childhood drawings of them. I learned your real name and the first letter of your surname. I was surprised when you told me about your brothers and sisters. Everything seemed to happen for the first time.

I re-listened to your voice messages and re-read your texts over and over. I wanted to know everything: your MBTI, what you were learning, photographing, watching and listening to, what you were doing, and what you would do in a random situation. I still keep all those feelings in my heart.

⌁Little projects, new words, photos, questions — and so much curiosity about you.
04
LATE SUMMER · AUTUMN · WINTER☄

My favourite part of the day was you

We started talking a lot, almost every evening after school. Time with you went by so quickly. I got to know your character better and discovered how amazing and kind you are. You always had the best ideas, like making websites. You sent funny stickers, and I collected them all. You joked so much, and I adored your jokes.

We finally played Asphalt together, then talked late into the night. We played games until midnight, searching for something else to play. You found Stumble Guys, and there was always a new update to try. We shared stories, looked at things together, and laughed. My happiness knew no limits.

I looked at your class notes and photos of your exams. I saved everything connected to you. We stayed on calls until morning, scrolled through our feeds together, and sometimes you turned on screen sharing just so I could see your face. When I was in a bad mood, I looked at your bright childhood photo.

I loved your Halloween jokes, even the silly dirty ones. Do you remember that Peppa Pig photo of me? We started making memes of each other and counting who had more. I was so nervous to send you the elephant photo and the other memes, even though I wanted you to laugh. We played Truth or Dare late at night, shared childhood photos, and watched each other grow up through old pictures.

I remember how worried I was about your birthday gift and how much I wanted to know your address so I could send you something. I remember your sister asking if you would date me — I was so surprised that my mood shot up instantly. And when she wanted to give me her number so we could talk? I could hardly believe any of it was happening.

I was shocked when you guessed where I lived. I loved seeing the beauty of India through your eyes. Everything you created, every new thing you learned, made me incredibly happy. You became such a huge source of energy in my life. I felt happy because of your messages, your photos, your memories, your answers — simply because they were from you.

In the first days of the new year, we finally saw each other's faces properly. I loved that photo where your face was barely visible because of the streetlight. I loved the avatar you suggested I use. When I lost my phone, I was so scared I would lose your number, your photos, and our chat. I was ridiculously happy when you sent your new photos again, along with all the dog stickers and your masterpieces.

Do you remember when we talked about the criteria for our future husband or wife? 😉 There was always something new to discover. I saw so much more of the world through you than I had seen in my first thirteen years.

“I wanted this happiness to never end.”
05
THE WORDS THAT STAYED♡

“You have such beautiful eyes 💖”

I still remember your first message: “I love you 😻 (in a friendly way).” Then you started giving me compliments. When you said, “You have such beautiful eyes 💖,” my happiness knew no limits. At first, I didn't believe it, but it felt so, so good.

After that, whenever I looked in the mirror at my own eyes, I remembered you and felt that same warmth. I was happy that I could compliment you too and tell you about my feelings. In this online little world, it felt like it was just us, together in our own space. I liked that so much.

And I still remember April 1st, when you wrote that you liked me. When I answered that I adored you, you decided not to tell me it had been a joke. Thank you for that. It feels like it happened almost yesterday, yet it's already a memory — one of the most pleasant memories I've ever had.

06
02 MAY 2026♡

CHAPTER 295 · THE DAY WE BECAME US

“Would you like to be my girlfriend?”

We were on a call at night, playing games, and then we finished. You were sharing your photos with me, as always. And then you wrote:

“I've been clear in my head for a while. I like you, and I don't want to keep this undefined anymore. Would you like to be my girlfriend??”

I froze. I thought maybe I was dreaming. I was in complete shock. I told you I was worried about what I would tell my parents, and you said, “Maybe this could be a secret between you and me.” I never thought you were thinking about me that way. I thought you saw me only as a friend.

I loved that you gave me time to think. It all felt unreal. Even after I agreed, I was still worried, so the next day I told you I wasn't worried anymore. And then I asked:

“Am I your girlfriend now?”

You answered with a better question — although, yes, we did have to fix that little typo:

“Am I your boyfriend now?”

And I answered, full of happiness:

“yes you are 🥳” ✦

And just like that, a story that began on July 11, 2025 became our relationship on May 2, 2026.

FOR YOU, ALWAYS

If I could be there right now…

I'd probably sit next to you and listen to you talk about your day. I'd ask you a hundred questions, even the random ones, and then somehow we'd end up laughing about something completely different.

I miss the calls, the games, your photos, your voice, your jokes, and all the tiny things that might not seem important to anyone else. I miss learning new things about you. I miss how one ordinary message from you could make an ordinary day feel special.

Sometimes I wish the distance were just a little smaller. But even with all these kilometres between us, you've become such a real and important part of my life. I carry our memories with me — the nervous first call, the late nights, the silly memes, the “I love you (in a friendly way),” April 1st, and the day we finally became us.

Thank you for being someone I can laugh with, learn from, tell things to, and miss this much. I don't know how to fit all of it into one letter. I only know that when I think about the memories I treasure most, you're in so many of them.

Until I can be next to you, here's a little reminder:
somewhere in this world, I'm thinking of you. ♡

yours, the girl who still remembers everything ♡
